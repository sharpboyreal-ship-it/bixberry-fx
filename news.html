What moves the forex market?

Learn about the major themes behind currency price movements.

CENTRAL BANKS

Interest Rate Decisions

Decisions and guidance from institutions such as the Federal Reserve, European Central Bank, and Bank of England can change currency-market expectations.

INFLATION

Inflation Reports

Inflation data can influence expectations about interest rates. Markets may react when results differ from forecasts.

EMPLOYMENT

Jobs and Economic Growth

Employment figures and economic growth reports help analysts assess economic conditions and possible policy changes.

GLOBAL EVENTS

Geopolitics and Risk

Political developments, trade disputes, and unexpected global events can affect investor confidence and demand for currencies.

How to analyse a news release

01. Check the source

Prefer official announcements and reliable reporting. Check the publication date and time.

02. Compare expectations

Compare the reported figure with forecasts and previous data. Consider revisions and broader context.

03. Manage your risk

News can trigger sudden price movements, wider spreads, and slippage. Avoid assuming that a headline guarantees a market direction.
