📚 Your learning path

LESSON 01

What is Forex?

Understand the world's foreign exchange market.

Forex means foreign exchange. It involves exchanging one currency for another. For example, EUR/USD represents the euro against the US dollar.

Currency prices can rise or fall due to economic news, interest rates, and market demand.

LESSON 02

Currency pairs

Learn to read pairs such as EUR/USD and GBP/USD.

The first currency is the base currency. The second is the quote currency.

If EUR/USD is 1.1000, one euro is worth 1.10 US dollars at that quoted rate. The rate available to you may differ.

LESSON 03

Risk management

Understand why protecting your money matters.

Forex trading can result in significant losses. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses.

Learn about position sizing, stop-loss orders, and risk-to-reward ratios before considering real trading. A stop-loss cannot guarantee a particular exit price in all market conditions.

LESSON 04

Charts and trends

Discover how traders study price movements.

Candlestick charts display price movement over a chosen period. Traders may study trends, support, resistance, and volatility.

Charts cannot predict the future with certainty. Always consider risk and avoid relying on a single indicator.
