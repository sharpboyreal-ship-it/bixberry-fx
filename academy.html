LESSON 01 · BEGINNER

What is Forex?

Understand the foreign exchange market and how currency trading works.

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the market where currencies are exchanged. Traders compare one currency with another, such as the euro against the US dollar. Currency prices can rise or fall, and trading can result in losses.
LESSON 02 · BEGINNER

Currency Pairs

Learn what EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY mean.

A currency pair compares two currencies. In EUR/USD, EUR is the base currency and USD is the quote currency. If EUR/USD is 1.1000, one euro is worth 1.10 US dollars at that quoted rate. Rates vary by provider and market conditions.
LESSON 03 · BEGINNER

Pips and Spreads

Understand two important terms used when discussing forex prices.

A pip is a standard unit used to describe price movement. For many pairs, it is 0.0001; for many JPY pairs, it is 0.01. The spread is the difference between the bid and ask prices. Actual trading costs may also include commissions and fees.
LESSON 04 · CHARTS

Candlestick Charts

Learn what candles show about price movement over time.

A candlestick represents an opening price, closing price, high and low for a selected period. A candle's body shows the difference between open and close. Its wicks show the high and low. Colors depend on the chart's settings.
LESSON 05 · ANALYSIS

Support and Resistance

Explore price areas traders watch on charts.

Support is an area where price has previously found buying interest. Resistance is an area where selling interest has appeared. These are zones, not guarantees: prices can break through them, and past behavior does not ensure future results.
LESSON 06 · RISK

Risk Management

Learn why protecting your trading capital matters.

Decide how much you can afford to lose before considering a trade. Position size, leverage, stop-loss orders and trading costs all affect risk. Stop-loss orders may not execute at the expected price during fast or illiquid markets. Never trade with money needed for essential expenses.
