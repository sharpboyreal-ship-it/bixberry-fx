Important Economic Indicators

These announcements can influence currencies. Their impact depends on market expectations, the published results, and wider economic conditions.

EMPLOYMENT

US Nonfarm Payrolls

Measures changes in US employment outside certain categories. Traders watch it for clues about economic strength and Federal Reserve policy.

INFLATION

Consumer Price Index

Tracks changes in consumer prices. Unexpected inflation figures can change expectations about future interest rates.

CENTRAL BANKS

Interest Rate Decisions

Decisions and statements from central banks can influence currency values through changes in interest-rate expectations.

ECONOMIC GROWTH

Gross Domestic Product

GDP measures the value of goods and services produced in an economy. Growth figures can influence expectations about economic health.

CONSUMER ACTIVITY

Retail Sales

Provides information about consumer spending. Strong or weak results can affect expectations for economic growth.

BUSINESS ACTIVITY

PMI Reports

Purchasing Managers' Index surveys provide information about activity in manufacturing and services. Readings are interpreted in context.

How to Read an Economic Release

1. Previous

The previously reported figure provides historical context.

2. Forecast

The forecast represents an expectation before the new data is released.

3. Actual

Compare the published figure with expectations. Markets may react differently depending on context.
