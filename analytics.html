📚 About your statistics

Win rate is the percentage of trades with a positive recorded result. Breakeven trades are neither wins nor losses. Net results include only the costs entered in your journal.

Because pairs can use different quote currencies, results are not added across currencies as if they were the same currency. Convert them to a common currency before comparing overall money values.

These statistics describe recorded practice trades; they do not predict future performance or guarantee profits.
